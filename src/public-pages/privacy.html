Himanshu XL Tools - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Himanshu XL Tools is an Excel productivity add-in. Workbook data is processed to provide the features requested by the user.

Data processing

The add-in may process workbook data required to perform selected Excel tools and dashboard operations.

Licensing

Licensing services may process license, activation and device information required to validate authorized use of the product.

Customer information

Information required for licensing, support and service administration is used for those purposes.

Security

Production communication is performed over HTTPS where supported by the service.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the Himanshu XL Tools support channel provided with the product.
